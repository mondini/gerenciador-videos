## 2. GANCHO E RESUMO

**Gancho (primeiros 15 s, como está no vídeo):**
> Maybe you already have more than you once dreamed of. A roof over your head. Light at the touch of a finger. Food you never had to hunt. And still... at night, something whispers... not yet. Not enough.

**Resumo:** Marcus Aurelius ruled the whole known world, and still, night after night, he wrote reminders to himself about not being ruined by what he already had. A slow, calm look at his private journal, a prayer from the book of Proverbs, and the quiet voice that keeps saying "not yet, not enough".

Marcus Aurelius ruled Rome for nearly twenty years, through plague and endless war on the Danube frontier. In a soldier's tent at the edge of the world, he kept a private journal in Greek that was never meant for us: the Meditations, a book whose title simply means "To Himself". In this slow, calm episode we read what the most powerful man alive wrote about simplicity, difficult people, and the danger of becoming "only the crown".

Then we cross to a much older book. A prayer in Proverbs asks for "neither poverty nor riches", and a single line warns that "pride goes before destruction". Two men who never met, centuries apart, arriving at the same quiet truth about having enough.
